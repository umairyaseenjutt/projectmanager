Career journey

From freelance motion design to creative project leadership.

My progression has been built through hands-on delivery, direct client ownership, project sales and increasingly complex creative production.

I started my professional journey in 2018 as a freelance Motion Designer and Video Editor, working directly with international clients and managing projects from initial brief through production, revisions and final delivery.

As my freelance work grew, the role expanded beyond execution into requirements gathering, proposals, project sales, production planning, client communication, feedback management and long-term relationship building. Through Fiverr and Upwork, I delivered work for clients across 22+ countries, completing 1,000+ Fiverr orders and 100+ Upwork projects while maintaining a 4.9 Fiverr rating, Top Rated status and a 100% Upwork Job Success Score.

I later progressed into Creative Project Management at MBA Studio, where I now manage SaaS demos, product videos, promotional campaigns, tutorials and marketing content from client acquisition and discovery through planning, team coordination, revisions, quality control and final delivery — while remaining hands-on with motion design, storyboarding and creative direction when needed.

My project portfolio includes SaaS, AI, fintech, healthcare and technology work for brands including ClinicHQ, Wondershare DemoCreator, CMTrading, AES International, PatientMD, Opius AI, Blufire and Doratoon. My academic background in Applied Psychology and ongoing MPhil in Public Relations & Advertising adds a practical perspective on communication, audience behavior, leadership and stakeholder relationships.
